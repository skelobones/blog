AIF is a revision of the Research Project subject that all Year 12s (17-18yo senior highschoolers) must complete. This is the stage in Australian school where we students get to make a lot of our own choices. I'm very excited for this subject!

>*"Students will begin this subject with varying levels of understanding of their individual agency, self-regulation, metacognition, reflective practice, and evaluative judgement..."*

The point of the subject is to train the ability to learn something by yourself (without a course, without teachers) and you're graded on how well you show the progress you made over a semester. I've been planning to use Quartz to show what I've done rather than a PowerPoint or a written report.

We have around 10 weeks for the project itself while the remaining 8 weeks are to collate natural evidence of learning (NEOL) into a timeline. We also have a reflection task after all that. 2 weeks in (not including school holidays) and I already have a lot of stuff to collect.
