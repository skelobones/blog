## The Precision of a Goal
People say "Split up very difficult goals into smaller manageable tasks!" but I believe that stage overlooks the importance of fully defining a goal. 

```mermaid
flowchart TD


origin("**I want to make a slot car**")

origin --> a(Scale model of a real car)
origin --> b(Overengineered optimised slot car)

a --> a1(Practise model painting skills)
a --> a2(Become more accurate)
a --> a3(Train speed at making models)

b --> b1(Very fast slot car)
b --> b2(Cheap, mainly 3d printed slot car)
b --> b3(Slot car suited for worn down tracks)

```
See how a single goal can mean so many different things?
