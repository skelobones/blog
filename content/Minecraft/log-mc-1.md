On the weekend I started a new Minecraft world with Create: Aeronautics installed. I truly **don't** have the time to begin a Minecraft world (nor to be doing this blog) but I wanted to write an example article that's also an update I can share with others.

Create: Aeronautics was released about a month ago now. It's a mod that's been 3 years in development and adds physics. I can make airships, airplanes, boats, cars... 

>[!quote] Jack "Jablinski" Black
>"Anything you dream of, you can make! Zero limits!"

And it's surprisingly survival friendly. Create (another mod released ~6y ago & major updated 2y ago) adds Andesite Alloy, which is the iron/cobble/wood essential for Create. 1 alloy is crafted from 2 Iron Nuggets and 2 Andesite. Thing is, you need a lot of it, which is what makes it a challenge.

With Create: Aeronautics, I can make physics-interacting structures with something called the Physics Assembler. It's crafted from a lever... 4 alloy? and Andesite Casing (made from andesite alloy and a stripped log) making it SUPER cheap.

My main challenge with Minecraft such far is making it fun to mine. I want to build a starter house, but I also want to get the base gear to do so. Thing is you can just straight up skip the starter level of the mod, go to the Nether for the 2<sup><small>nd</small></sup> stage's materials, and return home to get automation going.

>[!question]- What is Create..?
>Create as a base mod adds a bunch of automation and moving parts in case you haven't heard of it by now. Gulps in out of order

Boats are kind of hard in C:Aero (I'll refer to these addons as C:`addon` from now on) because water physics aren't perfect & propellers are quite slow. Airships are relatively easy. You need a Hot Air Burner & lots of Hot Air Envelopes to get enough lift. I got a wool farm going quite quickly, my younger sister played with me for a session and she was kind and got some sheep and a cow into a pen. I however... heh... accidentally crafted a whole stack of wool into "White Cushion" blocks from another mod instead of the cheaper Envelopes I actually needed... I'll probably make a bouncy castle at some point.

Thankyou for reading such far, I hope to continue my gameplay diary entries.
