I need all this wool to build 'windmills' for power generation. A lot easier than Steam Engines. I finished off the last play session with me setting up an iron farm then a creeper jumping me.

>[!faq]- Iron Farms
>Iron is generated through;
>1. Cobblestone being mined (with drills)
>2. Cobble crushed into Gravel using Create's Millstones
>3. Gravel being 'washed' with a chance of having an iron nugget or flint
>	1. Dispose of flint.
>	2. 'Washing' is a jet of air from a fan flowing through a water source.
>4. Nuggets into bars. finished!
